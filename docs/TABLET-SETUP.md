# Putting Speak Again on his tablet

SEEDSED. A one-page guide for the helper. Nothing here needs a computer.

## First install (about 5 minutes, on Wi-Fi)

1. On the iPad, open **Safari** and go to the app's address, e.g. `https://sannastheorc.github.io/speak-again/`.
2. Tap the **Share** button (a square with an arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Close Safari and open **Speak Again** from the Home Screen. Always open it from there from now on.
4. Tap **Set up now**. This downloads the 41 MB speech model once. Keep the app open until the dot
   turns green and it says "Listening works offline".
5. Start a practice and tap the speak button. When iPad asks for the **microphone**, tap **Allow**.
   If you use the mirror, allow the **camera** too.

After this it works anywhere, with no internet.

## Getting a natural voice (recommended, free)

The app talks with the iPad's own voices. The best ones sound close to Siri and work offline,
but iPad doesn't download them until you ask:

1. On the iPad, open **Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices › English**.
2. Pick a voice marked **Premium** or **Enhanced** (for example Ava, Zoe, Evan or Nathan) and tap
   the download button. Try a few with the play button and pick the warmest one for him.
3. Open Speak Again › **Settings › Voice** and tap the new voice. "Most natural (automatic)" also picks
   the best installed voice by itself.

While you're in Settings, **Voice speed › Slow** often feels gentler.

## Everyday use

He taps **Start today's practice**. That's it. Each day it builds about 10 minutes of practice:
a word he knows to start, new words, words due for review, extra work on his hardest sounds, and a
couple of his own words. If he wants a particular stage, **Choose a stage** is on the home screen.

## Helper tools (the "Helper" button)

- **His words:** type names of family and friends, his street, his dog, foods he likes, things he
  likes to say. They join his daily practice. If the app's listener doesn't know a word (often names),
  it says so, and he or you decide whether he said it.
- **Record your voice:** tap **● Record** next to a word, say it clearly, then pause. From then on the
  app plays *your* voice for that word instead of the computer's. A familiar voice helps. Start with
  his own words and the stage he's on.
- **Settings:** name, computer voice and speed, and the other options.

## His name

The first time it opens, Speak Again asks for his first name. It's optional, and you can type it
for him. It's used for greetings ("Hi, Stan!") and now and then in praise. You can change it under
Settings › Name. There is no account and no password.

## Getting updates

When a new version is published, the next time the app opens with internet it fetches the update in
the background. A banner then says **"A new version is ready"**. Tap **Update now**. Practice is
never interrupted by an update.

## Things that would erase his progress

Progress, his words and your recordings are saved only on that tablet. These would erase them:
- deleting the Speak Again icon from the Home Screen;
- Settings › Apps › Safari › **Clear History and Website Data**;
- the in-app Settings › **Erase progress**.

## If something isn't working

- **"The microphone is turned off for this app":** in iPad Settings, check the microphone is allowed
  for Speak Again or Safari websites, then reopen the app.
- **No voice when it reads the word:** check the volume buttons, and that Silent mode is off.
- **It never hears the word right:** that's the recognizer's limit, not him. Use **Hear myself** to
  listen back, and the helper button **"Helper: that was right"** when he said it.
- **Listening needs setting up again:** iPad sometimes clears stored data when storage is very
  full. Connect to Wi-Fi and tap **Set up now** again. His progress is stored separately.
