# Putting Speak Again on his tablet

SEEDSED. A one-page guide for the helper. Nothing here needs a computer.

## First install (about 5 minutes, on Wi-Fi)

1. On the iPad, open **Safari** and go to the app's address, e.g. `https://sannastheorc.github.io/speak-again/`.
2. Tap the **Share** button (a square with an arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
3. Close Safari and open **Speak Again** from the Home Screen. Always open it from there from now on.
4. Tap **Set up now**. This downloads the 41 MB speech model once. Keep the app open until the dot
   turns green and it says "Listening works offline".
5. Start a practice and tap the speak button. When iPad asks for the **microphone**, tap **Allow**.
   If you use the mirror, allow the **camera** too.

After this it works anywhere, with no internet.

## Getting updates

When a new version is published, the next time the app opens with internet it fetches the update in
the background. A banner then says **"A new version is ready"**. Tap **Update now**. Practice is
never interrupted by an update.

## Things that would erase his progress

Progress is saved only on that tablet. These would erase it:
- deleting the Speak Again icon from the Home Screen;
- Settings › Apps › Safari › **Clear History and Website Data**;
- the in-app Settings › **Erase progress**.

## If something isn't working

- **"The microphone is turned off for this app":** in iPad Settings, check the microphone is allowed
  for Speak Again or Safari websites, then reopen the app.
- **No voice when it reads the word:** check the volume buttons, and that Silent mode is off.
- **It never hears the word right:** that's the recognizer's limit, not him. Use **Hear myself** to
  listen back, and the helper button **"Helper: that was right"** when he said it.
- **Listening needs setting up again:** iPad sometimes clears stored data when storage is very
  full. Connect to Wi-Fi and tap **Set up now** again. His progress is stored separately.
